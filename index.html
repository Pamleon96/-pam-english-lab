1. Los 7 pronombres y sus auxiliares

PronombreTO BE hoyTO BE ayerSimple presenteSimple pasadoPerfecto

DO/DOES/DID se usan en preguntas y negaciones con verbos ordinarios; no sustituyen al verbo principal en afirmaciones normales. AM/IS/ARE/WAS/WERE sí son formas de TO BE.

2. ¿Cuándo utilizo cada tiempo?

IdeaCuándoAfirmaciónPregunta
Presente simpleRutina / gustoShe works.Does she work?
TO BE presenteEstado / profesiónShe is happy.Is she happy?
Presente continuoAhora mismoShe is working.Is she working?
Pasado simpleAyer / terminadoShe worked.Did she work?
TO BE pasadoEstado pasadoShe was happy.Was she happy?
Presente perfectoExperiencia / vínculo con hoyShe has worked here since April.Has she worked here since April?

“Today” no es un tiempo verbal: She works today (horario), She is working today (situación temporal), She has worked a lot today (lo hecho hasta ahora).

3. ¿IS o DOES? Elige el camino

IS / ARE / AM

Ser / estar: She is tired. → Is she tired?

Ahora, acción en curso: She is working. → Is she working?

No: Does she tired? / Does she working?

DO / DOES

Rutinas y gustos: She likes coffee. → Does she like coffee?

Acciones habituales: She works. → Does she work?

No: Is she like coffee? / Is she work?

5. La -S y sus excepciones

AFIRMATIVO · HE / SHE / IT

work → works
watch → watches
study → studies
have → has

PREGUNTA / NEGACIÓN

Does she work?
She doesn't watch TV.
Does he study?
She doesn't have a car.

La -S del presente simple NO se aplica a I / YOU / WE / THEY, ni después de DOES / DOESN'T. En pasado con DID, tampoco añadimos -ED al verbo principal.
